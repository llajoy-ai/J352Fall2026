The Neighbourhood Pt.2

Starting Fresh:

After releasing their three new songs the new album, "(((((ultraSOUND)))))+, was released on Nov. 14, 2025. The album featured 16 songs. Then four more songs were added to the delux version of the album on Feb. 20 of this year. Then, they quickly announced their World Tour, and the first night of the concert was on March 28, in Austin,Texas. The last night of the tour will be Oct. 9 and take place in Los Angeles, California. The band has traveled to North America, Canada, Europe, Australia, Asia and South America. In the first half of the tour, the openers were Noise Dept. and Neggy Gemmy. The second half of the tour features Noise Dept. and Nessa Barrett. Currently, the band is Mexico preparing to perform tomorrow the 23 at the Auditorio Banamex Monterrey. Since beginning their tour, they have also played at some music festivals. They played at Lollapalooza in Chicago, Osheaga in Canada and Bonnaroo Music + Arts Festival in Tennessee. Their World Tour features music from their new album as well as a variety of songs from past albums that fans love. For example, they are playing "Softcore", "Sweater Weather", "Reflections", "Daddy Issues", "Beach" and many other songs from their earlier albums. At each concert the band performs with fun colorful background visuals that make the tour more interesting. Since starting to tour the band has not experienced any additional backlack from fans regarding their decision to rehire the drummer Brendan Fried. Success for their newest album has only increase. However, the band has not hinted at any more music being released in the new future, especially since they are so busy touring for the first time together in 5 years.



















Concert Expectations:

At the concert, fans are able to shop for newly designed merch as they are waiting in line. The options include a hoodie with their classic upside-down house on it, and I shirt with the World Tour icon. Fans can also buy tote bags, lighters and a Hula Girl figureine in honor of one of the new songs on their album "Hula Girl". The Noise Dept. has toured with the band the entire schedule since the performer is actually a member of the band. One special element of the Noise Dept. opening is that he will play a song from the bands discography and mash it up in a fun new way that is interactive and exciting for the fans waiting for the performance. When the band comes on, they stay on stage for the full hour and a half that their set list lasts. There are no clothing changes and only breaks for the lead singer, Jesse Rutherford, to talk tothe audience and forthe band to have a quick water break. The concert opens with their New Song "Hula Girl" and ends with their classic fan favorite "Softcore". The band ends the show by playing their song "Red Flag" in the background as they say goodbye to fans. Overall, while the band has run into hiccups throughout their career, the music and fans have stayed true to them. Personally, as a fan of their music I think they evoke a lot of emotion and beautiful feeling in all of their songs. When I went to their concert at the Anthem in Washington D.C. on April 10 it was one of the best concert experiences I have ever had. Overall, I hope they continue to make music and better choices in the future.

The End
