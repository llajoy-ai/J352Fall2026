September 11, 2001

Key moments of the attacks on the World Trade Center in New York City, with the wider U.S. events of that morning included for context.
Historical context: This infographic is designed for education and remembrance. Times are approximate and follow Eastern Daylight Time (EDT). It avoids graphic imagery and focuses on the sequence of events and emergency response.
7:59 a.m.
Travel / context

American Airlines Flight 11 departs Boston

Flight 11 leaves Boston Logan International Airport for Los Angeles. It is later hijacked and flown toward New York.

8:14 a.m.
Travel / context

United Airlines Flight 175 departs Boston

Flight 175 leaves Boston for Los Angeles. It is also later hijacked.

8:46 a.m.
World Trade Center

Flight 11 strikes the North Tower

American Airlines Flight 11 crashes into the north face of the World Trade Center's North Tower (1 WTC), beginning the attack in New York City.

8:46 a.m.
Emergency response

Emergency response begins

Firefighters, police officers, emergency medical personnel and other responders move toward the World Trade Center complex as people begin evacuating.

9:03 a.m.
World Trade Center

Flight 175 strikes the South Tower

United Airlines Flight 175 crashes into the south face of the World Trade Center's South Tower (2 WTC), making clear that the first crash was part of a coordinated attack.

9:37 a.m.
National context

American Airlines Flight 77 strikes the Pentagon

Flight 77 crashes into the Pentagon in Arlington, Virginia. The attacks are now occurring at multiple locations.

9:59 a.m.
World Trade Center · response

South Tower collapses

The South Tower collapses after burning for less than an hour. The collapse sends a large debris and dust cloud through Lower Manhattan.

10:03 a.m.
National context

Flight 93 crashes in Pennsylvania

United Airlines Flight 93 crashes near Shanksville, Pennsylvania, after passengers and crew attempt to regain control of the hijacked aircraft.

10:28 a.m.
World Trade Center · response

North Tower collapses

The North Tower collapses, less than two hours after it was struck. Rescue and evacuation operations continue amid the devastation.

5:20 p.m.
World Trade Center · response

7 World Trade Center collapses

7 World Trade Center, a separate 47-story building in the complex, collapses after sustaining extensive damage and fires.
