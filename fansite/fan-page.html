The Neighbourhood

Career:


The band was formed in 2011 by Jesse Rutherford, Jeremey Freedman, Zach Abels, Mikey Margott and Brandon Fried. Since releasing their first album in 2013 called "I Love You", they have released four other albums, singles, special editions and compilations. Specifically, one song on their first album helped the band gain national recognition. That song is "Sweater Weather". The band has experimented with many different genres of music, however their music is most commonly described as a mix of pop, alternative and rock music. Their other albums include: Wiped Out!, Hard To Imagine The Neighbourhood Ever Changing, Chip Chrome & The Mono-Tones and their newest release (((((ultraSOUND)))))+. Excitement about their newest album release was extreme since the band was on hiatus from 2020, after they released Chip Chrome & The Mono-Tones, until they released their new album on Feb. 20, 2026. Most of the band members are originally from northwest of Los Angeles, Californa. Their home state is mentioned in many songs, and themes of their songs feature parts of the lead singer's, Jesse Rutherford,early family life. Some of these songs include: "Greetings From California", "Daddy Issues", "Tabacoo Sunburst" and "Baby Came Home 2/Valentines". In addition to commenting on childhood, the band's songs also have themes of heartbreak, struggling with fame and feeling lost/alone.

The Hiatus:

In 2022, The Neighbourhood's drummer, Brandon Fried, was accussed by Maria Zardoya from the band The Marias of innapropriate behavior. Brandon Fried admitted to the accusation and claimed the incident was due to substance abuse issues. The band then accounced that he was removed from the group, and they said that they were taking time off from pperforming, producing music and social media. The lead singer Jesse Rutherford branched off on his own during the hiatus and made three albums before the band announced they were coming back. His name on Spotify is Jesse and his three albums are "&", "GARAGEB&" and "Wanted?". The drummer, Brandon Fried, then went on a journey of recovery to better himself after the incident brought criticism from frusturated fans. Then in 2024, fans started to notice subtle hints that caused them to think the band was coming back together. The Neighbourhood officialy announced they were back together on Oct. 24, 2025 when they released three new songs. These songs were "OMG", "Lovebomb" and "Private" and were the first releases of their new album. However, some fans were not very happy when they found out the band would be returning with Brandon Fried. This led to debate over the new music, and some fans bashing the band for letting him continue in the band. However, the band did not back down from it's decision, and they still had large success with their newly released album.
